Privacy Policy.

Laikve Electrical respects your privacy and handles personal information provided through this website only for legitimate business purposes.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote or contact request, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, suburb or job location, type of work and details about the electrical work you require.

How we use your information

Information you provide is used to respond to enquiries, prepare or discuss quotes, arrange electrical work, communicate about jobs and maintain ordinary business records.

Website enquiry forms

Website quote requests are submitted through the website form service used by Laikve Electrical and are sent to Laikve Electrical for follow-up. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not necessary for your enquiry.

Disclosure of information

Personal information is not sold. Information may be handled by service providers used to operate the website, email, accounting or business systems, or disclosed where required by law.

Access and corrections

You may contact Laikve Electrical if you want to ask about personal information you have provided or request that inaccurate information be corrected.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact Laikve Electrical at taylor@laikveelectrical.com.au or call 0421 393 451.
